🔍 How are animals different?

🐠 Fish vs 🐦 Bird
Fish swim in water with fins. Birds fly in the air with wings.
🐒 Monkey vs 🐍 Snake
Monkeys have limbs to climb. Snakes have no legs – they slither.
🐻‍❄️ Bear vs 🐧 Penguin
Bears run fast on four legs. Penguins slide and waddle slowly.
On land vs In water
Land animals use legs or slide. Water animals use fins or tails.
